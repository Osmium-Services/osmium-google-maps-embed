**Google Maps Embed** shows a single place on a map. You create a named map here, and your site's design shows it wherever it is needed.

## Add a map

1. Press **New Map**.
2. Enter a **Map name**.
3. Enter a **Slug**: lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. Your site's design finds the map by its slug, so keep it short and do not change it later.
4. In Google Maps, find your place, choose **Share**, then **Embed a map**. Copy only the `src` value, which starts with `https://www.google.com/maps/embed`, into **Embed URL**.
5. Press **Save**.

After saving, the edit page shows a **Preview** so you can check the right place is showing.

## Change or remove one

Use **Edit** on the map's row. **Delete** moves it to **Deleted Maps**.
